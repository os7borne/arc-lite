# Arc Lite privacy policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026

Arc Lite does not collect, store on a server, sell, share or transmit any personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

## What stays on your device

Arc Lite uses browser features to work, and everything it reads or saves stays in your browser:

- **Tabs, tab groups, history, top sites, recently closed tabs and downloads** are read locally to power the command bar, start page, downloads chip and session restore.
- **Settings, your to-do list, your own background photos and the session snapshot** are saved in the browser's local extension storage (`chrome.storage`). Nothing is synced or uploaded.
- **Media on the page** (title, artist, artwork, play state) is read locally to show the mini player. It is not recorded or sent anywhere.
- **The URL you copy** goes to your clipboard only. The clipboard is read only when you run a command bar action that uses it, such as "Paste and go" or "Open URLs from clipboard", and what it holds is never stored or sent anywhere.
- **Which command bar results you pick** for what you type is kept in local extension storage, only to rank those results higher next time.
- **Snoozed tabs** (the address, title, scroll position and wake time of tabs you snooze) are kept in local extension storage until they wake or you delete them. A snoozed tab is closed, and reopened by an alarm in your browser; nothing is sent anywhere.
- **Focus mode** keeps its timer, your blocked-site list and your exceptions in local extension storage. Blocking is done by the browser's own redirect rules; Arc Lite does not record which sites you visit or try to visit. Desktop notifications, if enabled, appear only when a focus or break phase ends.
- **Thumbnails of finished image and video downloads** are made on your device from the downloaded file and kept in memory only. If the file can't be read, Arc Lite may re-request the original download address once, without sending anything about you beyond an ordinary web request.
- **Dragging a download onto a page** uses Chrome's debugger permission for an instant to hand that one file to the page you dropped it on, the same as dragging it in from your file manager. The debugger is detached straight afterwards. You can turn this off in settings.

## Network requests

The only requests Arc Lite makes are for the optional daily start-page photo, and only if you pick one of these sources: Bing, Wikipedia (Wikimedia), NASA or Picsum. These requests are sent without cookies or credentials and contain no information about you beyond what any web request includes, such as your IP address. If you use a gradient, a solid colour or your own photo, Arc Lite makes no network requests at all.

Arc Lite does not load or run remote code.

## Third parties

Arc Lite shares no data with third parties. The photo sources above receive only the request described above and have their own privacy policies.
