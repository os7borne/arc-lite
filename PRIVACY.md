# Arc Lite privacy policy

Last updated: 23 October 2026

Arc Lite does not collect, store on a server, sell, share or transmit any personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

## What stays on your device

Arc Lite uses browser features to work, and everything it reads or saves stays in your browser:

- **Tabs, tab groups, history, top sites, recently closed tabs and downloads** are read locally to power the command bar, start page, downloads chip and session restore.
- **Settings, your to-do list, your own background photos and the session snapshot** are saved in the browser's local extension storage (`chrome.storage`). Nothing is synced or uploaded.
- **Media on the page** (title, artist, artwork, play state) is read locally to show the mini player. It is not recorded or sent anywhere.
- **The URL you copy** goes to your clipboard only.

## Network requests

The only requests Arc Lite makes are for the optional daily start-page photo, and only if you pick one of these sources: Bing, Wikipedia (Wikimedia), NASA or Picsum. These requests are sent without cookies or credentials and contain no information about you beyond what any web request includes, such as your IP address. If you use a gradient, a solid colour or your own photo, Arc Lite makes no network requests at all.

Arc Lite does not load or run remote code.

## Third parties

Arc Lite shares no data with third parties. The photo sources above receive only the request described above and have their own privacy policies.
